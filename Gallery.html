Bharatanatyam costumes are colorful and elegant.
They are designed to highlight the dancer's movements and expressions.
Traditional accessories such as jewelry, flowers, and ankle bells complete the dancer's appearance.


Essential Dance Accessories

  • Temple jewelry
  • Salangai (ankle bells)
  • Waist belt
  • Head ornaments
  • Jasmine flowers
