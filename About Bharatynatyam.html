Bharatanatyam's origin can be traced to over 2,000 years ago in the temples of Tamil Nadu.
It was originally performed by temple dancers as a form of worship.
Today it is performed all over the world.

Bharatanatyam is one of the oldest classical dance forms of India.
It began in Tamil Nadu and is known for graceful movements
facial expressions and fascinating storytelling.

Interesting Facts about Bharatanatyam

  1. Name Origin:
    Bha = bhava or emotion,
    Ra = raga or melody
    Ta = tala or rhythm
    Natyam = dance
  2. Bharatyanatyam postures may mirror classic yoga poses
  3. Uses hand gestures called mudras.
    There are 28 single handed mudras and
    24 double handed mudras
