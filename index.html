Natyaratna was established in 2014 in State College to preserve and promote Bharatynatyam in the Kalakshetra tradition
It provides people of all ages intrested in dance in the Happy Valley community a meaningful foundation in indian classical arts.

Shreya Srinivasan is a dedicated Bharatynatyam dancer, choreographer and the artisitic director of Natyaratna.
She began her Bharatynatyam journey since her childhood under the guidance of Smt. Suman Patel
She has 15+ years experience and teaches students of different age groups. She has preformed her arangetram in 2010 and has over 40 students.

"I chose Bharatanatyam to express my artisitic inclination and talent.
It is also an important part of my Indian heritage and
I enjoyed learning about this traditional dance and sharing my knowledge with others."--- Shreya Srinivasan
