Bharatanatyam costumes are designed to make the dancer's movements clear and graceful.
The costumes are usually made with silk fabric and include traditional jewelry and ankle bells

Different Styles of Bharatanatyam Costumes

Pajama Style Costume: The pajama style costume has a loose-fitting pant design that allows dancers to perform difficult movements comfortably.
It is commonly used for stage performances because it provides flexibility and elegance.

Skirt Style Costume: The skirt style costume includes a long pleated skirt with a matching blouse and fan-shaped pleats in the front.
This style highlights the dancer's leg movements and traditional poses.

Dhoti Style Costume: The dhoti style costume is inspired by traditional Indian clothing.
It has a unique draped design that allows dancers to move freely while maintaining a classical appearance.


Common Costume Accessories

Accessory Purpose
Temple Jewelry Adds beauty and elegance to the dancer's appearance
Salangai (Ankle Bells) Creates rhythmic sounds during dance movements
Hair Decorations and Flowers Completes the traditional hairstyle and costume look
Traditional Makeup Makes facial expressions visible during performances
Silk Costume with Pleats Helps highlight body movements and classical poses

Key Features of Bharatanatyam Costumes

  1. Bright silk fabrics and colorful designs
  2. Fan-shaped pleats for graceful movements
  3. Matching blouse and decorative borders
  4. Traditional hair styles with flowers
  5. Jewelry and makeup for stage performances
